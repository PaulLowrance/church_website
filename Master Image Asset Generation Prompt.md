## Master Image Asset Generation Prompt
Generate a cohesive set of image assets for Brentwood Hills Primitive Baptist Church, a Primitive Baptist church in East Fort Worth, Texas. The visual identity should be warm, reverent, and editorial — like a church hymnbook or printed sermon rather than a tech app.

### Brand palette
- Primary accent: burgundy #6f2e2a
- Secondary accent: gold #b08a3e
- Light background: warm paper #fbf8f3
- Dark background: rich ink #1c1a17
- Use these colors consistently across all assets. Favor burgundy and gold on paper backgrounds, or paper/gold text on burgundy backgrounds.

### Design elements
- DO NOT use crosses as decorative or symbolic elements.
- DO use: communion bread and wine, an open Bible, a basin and towel for foot washing, wheat, grapes, open hands, light from a window, an empty pulpit, a simple wooden table, a hymnal, or candlelight.
- Style should be minimal, calm, and hand-crafted: soft linework, muted textures, subtle gradients, or warm photography. Avoid neon, neon-purple, glossy 3D, or corporate tech aesthetics.
- Text, if any, should be minimal, elegant, and readable. Prefer no text on images except the logo/wordmark.
### Assets needed
1. Church logo / wordmark (SVG primary, 1024×256 PNG fallback)
    - Horizontal wordmark: "Brentwood Hills Primitive Baptist Church"
    - Use a refined serif for the church name and a small sans-serif tagline: "East Fort Worth, Texas"
    - Incorporate small symbolic mark: open Bible, communion cup, or wheat sheaf beside the text.
    - Deliver on transparent background and on burgundy background.
2. Sermon cover art master (1:1, 1024×1024)
    - Generate 5–10 unique square cover backgrounds that can be reused per sermon.
    - Each should feel like a small liturgical painting or photograph.
    - Themes:
    - Bread and wine on a wooden table
    - Open Bible with warm window light
    - Basin and towel, foot washing
    - Wheat and grapes, harvest/gathering
    - Empty pulpit with a single candle
    - Leave the center area relatively simple so sermon titles can be overlaid if needed, but do not put text on the image itself.
3. Homepage hero photograph (16:9, 1920×1080)
    - Warm, inviting interior or exterior of a small church building.
    - Soft morning light through windows.
    - Include subtle symbolic elements: wooden pulpit, open Bible, communion tray.
    - Leave the left 40% of the image darker and less detailed so hero text ("This Sunday's Sermon", title, buttons) can be placed there.
    - No people, no crosses.
4. Open Graph / social share image (1200×630)
    - Burgundy or paper background with the church wordmark at top.
    - Center: a 300×300 square area for the sermon cover image to be composited in.
    - Subtle border or gold line accent.
    - Leave lower third open for text overlay (sermon title, speaker).
5. Favicon set
    - favicon.svg: simple symbolic mark: open Bible or communion cup in burgundy.
    - favicon.png (32×32): same mark.
    - apple-touch-icon.png (180×180): same mark on rounded paper background.
    - maskable-icon.png (512×512): same mark with safe padding.
6. Default sermon cover placeholder (1024×1024)
    - Soft burgundy or paper background with a subtle open Bible / bread and wine motif.
    - Used when no cover image is uploaded.

### Output format

- Source files: PNG or SVG with layers where possible.
- Photographic assets: provide AVIF and WebP optimized versions at 1024, 512, and 256 widths.
- All assets should be suitable for both light and dark theme contexts or provide light/dark variants.

### Tone
Quiet, reverent, warm, invitation-not-marketing. "An invitation to worship" rather than "a brand launch."
